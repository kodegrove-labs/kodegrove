Meet YantraFox

KodeGrove is excited to introduce YantraFox a powerful collection of browser-based digital tools designed to make everyday online tasks faster, simpler and more accessible. From PDF management and developer utilities to AI-powered content creation, SEO optimization, financial calculators and image processing, YantraFox brings essential digital productivity tools together in one convenient destination.

Everything You Need in One Place

  • Powerful PDF Utilities: Merge, split, compress, rotate, convert and extract text from PDF documents.
  • Developer Productivity: Format JSON, generate UUIDs, work with Base64, calculate hashes and simplify everyday coding tasks.
  • AI-Powered Creativity: Generate professional emails, SEO descriptions, blog titles and content using Gemini-powered AI tools.
  • SEO & Digital Marketing: Build XML sitemaps, generate robots.txt, preview Google SERP snippets and create structured Schema.org markup.
  • Image & Media Processing: Resize, compress, crop and convert images directly in your browser.
  • Smart Financial Calculators: Calculate EMIs, GST, SIP returns, currency conversions and more.
  • Privacy-First Architecture: Document and file-processing operations run locally in your browser, reducing the need to upload private files to remote processing servers.
